# Brand Asset Guide

All three assets below were supplied by the project owner. The original PNGs are retained in this folder.

| File | Supplied role | Dimensions | Format |
|---|---|---:|---|
| `the-gladius-logo.png` | Official The Gladius emblem | 2048 × 2041 | PNG / RGBA |
| `university-logo.png` | Universitas Katolik De La Salle Manado logo | 220 × 220 | PNG / RGBA |
| `faculty-logo.png` | Faculty logo / identity artwork supplied by owner | 1378 × 1962 | PNG / RGBA |

## Handling rules

- Keep the supplied files unchanged as master assets.
- Preserve transparency and original colors.
- Use `object-fit: contain`, not `cover`.
- Keep the Gladius emblem as the dominant collective mark.
- Keep university and faculty marks as supporting institutional identities.
- Do not invent an official name or extra symbolism for the faculty artwork.
- Do not merge the three files into a new lockup image; compose them in the interface with proper spacing.
- Optimized web derivatives may be created only if a visual comparison confirms that no meaningful detail or transparency is lost.

The Gladius emblem file has an alpha channel. A preview against black does not mean the PNG has an opaque black background.
