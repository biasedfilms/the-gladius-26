# Content Integrity — The Gladius 26

`CONTENT-AND-FACTS.md` and the approved copy in `PRD.md` are the only source of truth for the initial release.

## Confirmed facts

- The Gladius 26 represents the 2026 Faculty of Engineering students at Universitas Katolik De La Salle Manado.
- The Gladius 26 was born from The Gladiators 23.
- The collective describes The Gladiators as its parents and The Gladius as their offspring.
- Gladius was chosen as a reference to the Gladiators' sword; the name represents the connection and inherited *warisan*/*genetika*.
- The emblem meanings are Pedang, Helm, Rantai, Perisai, and Roda Gear, as written in the PRD.
- Four shields represent the four Faculty of Engineering study programs in unity.
- The gear's 26 teeth represent the 2026 Faculty of Engineering cohort.

## Hard prohibitions

Never invent or imply:

- Exact founding dates, events, places, or a naming ceremony.
- Names of founders, leaders, students, or other people.
- Names of the four study programs.
- Achievements, awards, membership numbers, or institutional endorsements.
- New historical explanations for The Gladiators 23.
- New symbolic meanings for the sword, helmet, chain, shields, or gear.
- The name or biography of the faculty character artwork unless confirmed.

## Writing rules

- Primary language is natural Bahasa Indonesia.
- Preserve names and capitalization: The Gladius 26, The Gladiators 23.
- Keep *warisan* and *genetika* as the collective's metaphor for inherited identity/legacy; do not make it sound like a biological genealogy claim.
- You may improve grammar and rhythm only if the original meaning stays intact.
- Separate proposed tagline/editorial wording from official facts.
- Do not add filler paragraphs just to fill a design layout. Use whitespace instead.
- When a fact is missing, omit it or mark it for owner confirmation; never guess.

## Final content audit

Before completion, compare every historical and emblem statement on the site against `CONTENT-AND-FACTS.md`. Report any copy that has been added beyond the confirmed source.
