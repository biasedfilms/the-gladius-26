# Content & Facts — The Gladius 26

This file is the factual source of truth for all visible website copy. If a fact is not listed as confirmed, do not invent it.

## Confirmed identity

- Name: The Gladius 26.
- Identity: A collective representing the 2026 Faculty of Engineering students.
- Institution: Universitas Katolik De La Salle Manado.
- Faculty: Fakultas Teknik.
- Origin: The Gladius 26 was born from The Gladiators 23.
- Relationship: The Gladiators are described as The Gladius' parents; The Gladius are their offspring.
- Inheritance: The Gladius was meant to carry forward the *warisan* and *genetika* of The Gladiators, in the collective's metaphorical sense of inherited legacy and identity.
- Name: Gladius was chosen because it refers to the Gladiators' sword.

Diambil dari filosofi mahasiswa Teknik yang solid, satu tujuan dan satu arah. Terdapat 26 mata gear yang mengartikan Angkatan 26 Mahasiswa Fakultas Teknik — The Gladius.
