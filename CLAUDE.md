# The Gladius 26 — Project Instructions

This repository builds the The Gladius 26 Digital Identity Archive.

## Always follow

- Read `PRD.md`, `CONTENT-AND-FACTS.md`, and `design/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md` before major implementation work.
- Treat the supplied three brand assets as authoritative source files. Preserve their colors, proportions, transparency, and artwork.
- The Gladius 26 is the 2026 Faculty of Engineering collective at Universitas Katolik De La Salle Manado; it was born from The Gladiators 23.
- Do not invent dates, names, study-program names, founders, achievements, activities, quotations, or historical details.
- Keep the website in Bahasa Indonesia unless an English translation is explicitly requested.
- Follow the Cinematic Heritage × Modern Editorial art direction and the brighter, visible royal-blue palette defined in `design/design-tokens.css`.
- The Gladius emblem is the primary brand mark. University and faculty marks are supporting institutional assets and must retain their original colors.
- The hero headline is oversized `THE GLADIUS`; display `26` separately. Do not modify the supplied logo.
- Do not create generic SaaS layouts, repetitive cards, random gradients, decorative tech motifs, or motion on every element.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences, keyboard access, contrast, and mobile layouts.
- Keep this MVP static and focused. No database, login, CMS, admin panel, or unrelated features.

## Skills

Use the project skills in `.claude/skills/` when their scope applies. The most relevant skills are `frontend-design`, `brand-system`, `cinematic-motion`, `content-integrity`, `no-ai-slop`, `responsive-accessibility`, and `visual-qa`.

## Quality expectations

Inspect the repository before editing. Plan and critique the visual direction before coding. Implement fully, inspect rendered output where tooling permits, test key responsive widths, run lint/typecheck/tests/build when available, and report only checks that were actually run.
