# Design System — The Gladius 26

## Creative direction

**Cinematic Heritage × Modern Editorial.** A contemporary identity publication built around the real Gladius emblem and confirmed generational story. Use a visible royal-blue foundation, alternating silver chapters, restrained metallic gold, oversized editorial type, and one well-orchestrated hero motion sequence.

## Color tokens

See `design-tokens.css` and `design-tokens.json` for implementation tokens.

- Main blue surface: `#0E3670`.
- Alternate blue: `#174985`.
- Deep contrast only: `#061A35` / `#0A2852`.
- Gold accent: `#D4A246`; bright gold: `#E7C36E`.
- Silver section: `#DDE2E9`; light silver: `#F0F3F7`.
- Text on blue: `#F8FAFC`.
- Text on silver: `#152033`.

The main blue must read as blue, not near-black. Gold is an accent. Check real contrast for every text size; use off-white for small text where gold fails contrast.

## Three marks, three roles

1. The Gladius emblem is the primary collective mark and hero artwork.
2. The university logo is an institutional endorsement/affiliation mark.
3. The faculty logo/identity artwork is a faculty affiliation mark.

Keep source PNGs untouched. Maintain aspect ratio and transparency. Do not recolor, redraw, crop, or combine the marks into a new unofficial logo. Place the university and faculty marks together in a quiet institutional lockup; they should never compete with the Gladius emblem or oversized headline.

## Typography

- Display: **Bodoni Moda**, chosen over Cormorant Garamond after testing in the hero composition.
- Text and UI: **Manrope**.
- **IBM Plex Mono**, one weight, only for chapter markers and measured annotations.

## Layout rules

- Editorial grid, strong asymmetry, and intentional whitespace.
- Avoid a generic card dashboard.
- Use silver sections as breathing spaces between blue narrative chapters.
- Use large type as content, not merely decoration.
- Keep paragraph measure around 60–75 characters.
- Keep the main content width around 1240–1320px.
- Mobile gutters around 20–24px; desktop gutters adapt to viewport.
- Never sacrifice logo visibility or text contrast for visual effects.

## Signature interaction

The hero is the one primary cinematic moment: oversized title reveal → separate `26` edition marker → Gladius emblem reveal → university/faculty affiliation lockup. Keep it short and skippable. Other motion should be sparse and narrative-led.

## Left out on purpose

- Circuit boards, HUDs and other technology motifs unrelated to the content.
- Identical cards for every section.
- Constant background motion or parallax everywhere.
- Decorative fake Latin or unsupported heritage claims.
- Repeated fade-up on every element.
